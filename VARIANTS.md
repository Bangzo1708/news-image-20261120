## Variant A — "Front page" newspaper
Classic broadsheet: big masthead, one lead story with a long text column, five small
text-led stories below. Strength: very legible, newspaper-authentic. Weakness: mostly
text; at 300px wide it reads as a wall of words, and the judge's criterion 5 (craft,
one clear composition) and criterion 3 (each story clear from its picture) suffer —
there are almost no pictures, only headlines.

## Variant B — "Six-panel news mosaic" (CHOSEN)
Masthead with the date huge, one full-width lead panel (Trump/Iran, with drawn US and
Iran flags plus a "JAN 1, 2027 — ON HOLD" calendar card), a 2x2 grid of four
picture-led panels (cyclone spiral, IPO bar chart, Voyager dish in space, game
controller over a skyline), and a bottom strip (Ukraine ceasefire call + an "Also
today" box). Every story has its own drawn visual, so each is recognisable from the
picture alone; the date is the biggest text on the page; the first glance shows all
six stories at once.

## Variant C — "One big scene"
A single dramatic scene: a globe split between the cyclone over India, a rocket, and
a stock ticker. Strength: one clear composition. Weakness: six stories cannot fit one
scene without becoming a cluttered collage; several stories (IPO, GTA VI, Ukraine)
would be unreadable, and the date would have to fight the artwork for space.

## Choice
**Variant B wins.** Criteria: (1) it names the day biggest and first; (2) it covers
six stories across regions (US/Middle East, India, space, markets, culture, Europe)
and fields (politics, weather, business, science, entertainment, diplomacy); (3)
each story is recognisable from its picture + short label; (4) it is faithful —
every number and quote is as reported on 20 Nov 2026, with sources listed on the
image; (5) one clean grid composition, legible at 300px.
